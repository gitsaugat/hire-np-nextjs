If you have looked at HR software in Nepal in the last two years, you have probably come across TalentSathi. It is one of a small number of local platforms aimed at Nepal companies, and it covers a useful slice of the HR workflow.

This post is the honest comparison: where TalentSathi fits, where HireNP fits, and how to think about choosing between them.

## The category overlap

TalentSathi and HireNP are not really the same kind of product, which is the first thing worth saying out loud. TalentSathi is a broader HR platform — payroll, attendance, leave management, with hiring as one of several modules. HireNP is a focused hiring intelligence platform — screening, interview analysis, scheduling, offers, with deep AI on every step.

That overlap matters because it determines which tool wins for which job.

## Where TalentSathi is the right call

If your primary need is post-hire HR operations — running payroll for a Nepal team, tracking attendance, managing leave balances, generating payslips — TalentSathi is built for that. The hiring module exists, but it is a tracking system in the traditional sense rather than an intelligence layer.

If you already have a working hiring process and what you actually need is the HRMS underneath it, TalentSathi (or any of its Nepal HRMS peers) is a sensible choice. Pair it with whatever you use to source and screen.

## Where HireNP is the right call

If your primary pain is the hiring process itself — too many resumes to read, too many days to close, too many great candidates getting away — HireNP is built for exactly that pain.

The differences show up at every stage:

- **Screening.** HireNP's AI reads every resume and scores it against your specific job requirements with full reasoning. A traditional ATS module makes you read every resume manually.
- **Interview analysis.** HireNP's AI joins your Google Meet, transcribes the conversation, and scores it across communication, technical depth, and cultural alignment. Most HRMS hiring modules have no equivalent.
- **Scheduling.** Natural-language scheduling removes the back-and-forth chain. Most HRMS modules give you a calendar view, not coordination.
- **Offers.** AI-generated, Nepal Labour Act 2074-compliant offer letters with digital signing on the Business plan.

If hiring is the bottleneck in your business, the depth on hiring is what matters.

## The "use both" pattern

For Nepal companies past 30 employees, the cleanest pattern we see is to use both kinds of tools — but each one for what it is actually good at.

- **HireNP** for everything from "we need to hire" to "they signed the offer."
- **Your HRMS of choice** (TalentSathi, or any of its Nepal peers) for everything from "they signed the offer" to ongoing payroll, leave, and performance.

The handoff is clean: at offer signature, the structured candidate data leaves HireNP and lands in your HRMS as a new employee record.

## Pricing comparison

TalentSathi pricing varies by module and company size, typically structured as a monthly per-employee fee for the HRMS modules.

HireNP pricing is structured around hiring volume:
- Free: NPR 0 — 1 active job, 20 candidates per job
- Pay Per Job: NPR 4,000 — full workflow for one role
- Pro: NPR 19,900 per 30 days — 10 active jobs, full AI suite
- Business: NPR 26,600 per 30 days — unlimited jobs, Nepal Labour Act compliance

The 15-day free trial requires no card and gives you full access to the chosen plan.

## How to choose

A short decision tree:

- **Are you hiring more than two roles per quarter?** HireNP is the right hiring layer.
- **Do you have more than 20 employees on payroll?** A dedicated HRMS (TalentSathi or peer) is worth the spend.
- **Both?** Use both, with HireNP as the front of the funnel and your HRMS as the post-hire system of record.

The mistake to avoid is using either category to do the other category's job. An HRMS hiring module will not give you AI screening at the depth you need. A hiring platform will not run your payroll. Build the stack from what each tool is genuinely best at.

## What to test

If hiring is your bottleneck right now, start a 15-day HireNP trial and run your next role through it. You will know within a week whether AI screening, AI interview analysis, and automated offers fix the pain.

If post-hire HR operations is your bottleneck, evaluate TalentSathi against its Nepal peers for that specific job. Choose based on payroll accuracy, leave workflow, and reporting — not on hiring features.

Both decisions become easier when you stop trying to force one tool to do both jobs.
