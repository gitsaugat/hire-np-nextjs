For most of the last two decades, the default move for any growing Nepal company that needed to hire fast was the same: call a recruitment agency. The agency would send resumes, you would interview, you would hire, and you would pay them roughly one to two months of the hire's salary as a fee.

That model is breaking in 2026. Not because agencies stopped being useful, but because the work they actually do — sourcing, screening, scheduling — is the exact work that AI is now genuinely good at.

## What you are actually paying an agency for

Before you can replace something, you have to be honest about what it does. A typical agency engagement in Nepal looks like this:

- **Sourcing** — they post on Merojob, LinkedIn, and search their database.
- **Screening** — they read resumes and run a short phone call.
- **Shortlisting** — they send you three to five candidates.
- **Coordination** — they schedule your interviews.

Roughly 70% of an agency's billable value sits in steps two and three. Those are the steps that AI screening and AI interview analysis automate end-to-end.

## The replacement playbook

Companies making the switch are following a fairly consistent sequence. Here is what it looks like in practice:

**Step 1: Stop renewing the retainer.**

Most Nepal agency contracts are retainer plus success-fee. If your retainer is up for renewal, do not renew. Move that budget into your hiring software line item instead.

**Step 2: Move sourcing in-house.**

Posting on Merojob, Kumari Job, and LinkedIn is something your HR coordinator can do in 20 minutes per role. You do not need an agency for this. Use the multi-channel post features in your hiring platform to push to all of them at once.

**Step 3: Let AI do the first read.**

This is the step that used to take a recruiter four hours per 100 resumes. With HireNP, every resume gets scored against your specific job requirements in under a minute, with the AI's reasoning shown for each. Your HR coordinator now reviews 10 well-explained recommendations instead of 100 raw PDFs.

**Step 4: Use AI to sit in interviews.**

Most hiring leaks happen in the gap between "candidate seemed strong" and "we cannot remember exactly what they said." When the AI joins your Google Meet, transcribes the conversation, and scores it against your role's rubric, the gap closes. You can revisit every interview as data, not memory.

**Step 5: Automate scheduling and offers.**

These are pure coordination cost. Natural-language scheduling and AI-generated offer letters remove the back-and-forth that used to eat half your hiring manager's week.

## The math

For a single mid-level engineering hire in Nepal, an agency fee runs roughly NPR 80,000 to NPR 200,000 depending on seniority. One hire pays for an entire month of HireNP's Business plan, which gives you unlimited jobs and unlimited candidates.

For most companies the breakeven is the first hire of the month. Everything after that is pure margin recaptured.

## Where agencies still help

This is the part of the conversation most "replace your agency" articles skip. Agencies are still genuinely useful for:

- **Senior executive search** (CXO-level, where the relationship and discretion matter)
- **Confidential replacements** (when you cannot post publicly)
- **Highly specialized international hires** where your own network is thin

For everything else — your engineers, your sales reps, your operations team, your designers — the playbook above wins on both cost and speed.
